---
name: sharpen
description: "Use for /sharpen, or before starting a task that is vague, over-specified, or open-ended: find the expert approach, what the user is really after, and the right level of specification."
---

Before starting, think silently: how a top expert would approach this (real sources and methods), what the user is really after, and whether the task is over- or underspecified. Loosen constraints that don't serve the goal; leave choices that are the user's to make open.
Then do the work. Don't hand back a rewritten prompt, and don't narrate the framing.
Keep one independent check that the result is right (a test, a source comparison, an independent recomputation); when none is possible, say what stays unverified.
If a missing choice is the user's to make and blocks the work, ask one question.
