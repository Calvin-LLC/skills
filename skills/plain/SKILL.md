Restate your last message. Drop the jargon, aliases, and codenames; use the real names or describe the thing in words. Say it more simply and concisely, like one person talking to another, then end with what works now, how it reaches the goal, and the one next step.

This is the only restate skill. In Hermes, `quick_commands.bro` aliases `/bro` to this skill; everywhere else it runs as `/plain`.
