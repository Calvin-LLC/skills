Restate in your own words what you think the user's goals are and what problem they're trying to solve.

When the restatement does not gate the work, restate briefly, then proceed. When it gates the work ("confirm this with me before you begin"), prefer your harness's question tool (with none, ask in plain text and end the turn); ask the single confirming question and start nothing until the user answers.
